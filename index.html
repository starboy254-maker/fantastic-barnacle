Gold (XAU/USD) 5-Minute Scalping

Tool: Purra Buy Sell Signals on TradingView.

  • Green "Buy" label below candleLook for LONG
  • Red "Sell" label above candleLook for SHORT
  • Best sessionLondon / Asian overlap
  • AvoidMajor news + late NY
  • Stop lossJust beyond recent swing
  • Target1:1 to 1:2 risk-reward
Gold moves fast. Spread and slippage can eat a 5-minute scalp. Use a low-spread broker.

Adrian the Baddest Currency Strength

Adrian the Baddest is our name for the currency strength meter. On TradingView, search for the real tool: Currency Strength Meter [ForexCracked] or Currency Strength Meter – Closed Bar.

  • Long sideStrongest currency
  • Short sideWeakest currency
  • Best timeframe5m / 15m
  • Velocity trigger1.0+ point in one 5m bar
  • Spread readingNarrow = no trade
  • AvoidHigh-impact news (CPI etc.)
"Adrian the Baddest" tells you which pair to trade. It does not tell you when to enter. Use Purra or Composite for timing.
